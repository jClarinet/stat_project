Let's order the steps for a simple random sample!
Label the 9 plants with unique numbers from 1 to 9.
Write numbers 1 to 9 on identical slips of paper and place them in a hat.
Mix the slips thoroughly to ensure equal probability of selection.
Select 4 slips one at a time, without replacement, to form your sample.
